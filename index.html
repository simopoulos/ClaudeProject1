FAQ

Common questions

We're open Monday–Thursday 8am–7pm, Friday 8am–5pm, and Saturday 7am–5pm. We're closed on Sundays. Extended weekday hours until 7pm make it easy to book around your work schedule.

Yes — we accept most major dental insurance plans and will direct-bill your insurance provider. We also accept cash and debit. Contact our front desk to confirm coverage before your appointment.

Invisalign treatment at Art of Smile typically ranges from $4,000–$7,000, depending on complexity. This includes all check-in appointments every 6–8 weeks throughout treatment. Flexible payment plans are available — ask us about financing options.

Yes. We offer sedation options for anxious patients and for procedures like wisdom tooth extractions and oral surgery. Our team will review your health history and recommend the appropriate level of sedation for your procedure.

Your first visit includes a comprehensive exam, digital X-rays, a professional cleaning, and a consultation with your dentist. We'll review your dental history, discuss any concerns, and create a personalized care plan. Plan for about 90 minutes.

Yes — free parking is available for patients. We're located at 2527 Yonge St, Main Floor, north of Eglinton. We're also steps from the Eglinton subway station and Crosstown LRT stop.

We accommodate dental emergencies as quickly as possible. Call us at (416) 932-9179 and let the front desk know it's urgent. We do our best to fit emergency patients in the same day or next morning.

You can book by calling (416) 932-9179 Monday–Saturday during clinic hours, or by using the contact form below. New patients are always welcome — mention you're new and we'll make sure your first visit is smooth.
