Our philosophy

About Us

Art of Smile was built on the belief that exceptional dental care and a comfortable experience go hand in hand. Every visit should feel personal, calm, and confidence-building.

Since 2001, Dr. Battaglin and her team have been treating their Midtown Toronto neighbours with the same care they'd give their own family — using the most advanced technology available, in a warm, welcoming environment. We accept insurance, cash, and debit. Free Wi-Fi always on.
